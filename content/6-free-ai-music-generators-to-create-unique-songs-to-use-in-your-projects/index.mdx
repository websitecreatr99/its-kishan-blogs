Every day brings some new, exciting things we learn that artificial intelligence is able to do. There's an already very long list of interesting uses for ChatGPT alone—making travel itineraries and cracking tricky math problems. What it can't currently do is create music from a text prompt, but that doesn't mean other AI tools aren't up to the task.

These free AI music generators will piece together a brand new song based on just a few inputs from you. The result with every track is unique to your choices; every app has different ways you can customize to make sure that it truly feels like yours. Ready to get started as the conductor for an orchestra of bots?

## 1. Beatoven.ai (Web): Simplest Free AI Music Generator

One of the easiest free AI music-making apps has got to be Beatoven. Sign up, and straight away you can create a new song by giving it a track title, selecting the song length — it can be up to 15 minutes — and setting the tempo, which can be slow, medium, or fast. Then select a genre or regional sound.

Now, select the type of emotion you feel that you would like your song to have. Clicking on the play button for each card will give you a feel for which style of music you can expect, and after you have made your choice, it might take the AI a few minutes to produce new tracks using existing samples to match your choices.

You'll land in the main timeline editor from there, where you could play the AI-generated song and further edit by removing instruments, breaking the song into sections, and changing the emotion, genre, and tempo if the one you've selected doesn't satisfy you. You can change and hear alternate track options.

Once you have made changes, you will need to click Compose Track for the changes to take effect. Once truly satisfied with your final product, you can download it absolutely free and use it any way you want to.

Note that you'll have to give attribution to Beatoven, but you are free to use it for commercial projects too. On the free tier, though, you are restricted to only 15 minutes of downloads per month, while the paid tier increases that time to 60 minutes of download with the added ability to collaborate with a user. Premium users don't need to attribute to Beatoven.


## 2. Soundful (Web): AI Music With Custom Key, Notes, and Beats Per Minute

Like most other AI music generators, Soundful does its song creation based on two prime metrics: genre and mood. The moods are relatively abstract and change based on the genre you pick, so you might need to try a few permutations and combinations before hitting the right zone.

After having found the zone, Soundful asks you to customize the song based on two factors. First is the beats per minute, which configures the tempo for the music track. The second factor is the notes and keys. You can configure the basic note for the song (C, D, E, F, G, A, B) and whether you want it in a major or minor key.

Soundful further allows one to create loops for those small tracks that act as the perfect background music while having a short video. You can see their library of templates, which will inspire you or see the global tracks added by others.

The free version of Soundful has the availability of downloading ten created songs a month. However, a user will need to attribute Soundful for the Music Copyrights anywhere one will use the Track.

## 3. Ecrett (Web): Control Each Instrument in AI-Generated Tracks

Ecrett offers granular control over its AI-generated musical tracks, which one will rarely find elsewhere. It can nearly take you close to the manual free music generators for creating your songs.

First, choose the scene, which might be an adventure, fashion, or even travel; it might be any kind of mood—one happy, dark, chill, or another musical genre. Ecrett will arrange the base track according to the choices, over which you can control a tempo and volume, and change the length with immediate effect.

Now, here's the interesting part: each instrument in the song is a track. You can view a timeline of how that instrument plays. You can click on these blocks to let you make changes in how the instrument works throughout the song, to further customize beyond what the AI happened to offer. Ecrett also offers an option to upload a video to the editor window, so you get an idea of what the song will look like when matched with the video.

To download the generated track, you'll need to log in to its "big brother," Soundraw. Meanwhile, to be able to actually use it in your projects, you'll be required to subscribe to the paid plans; though to its merit, Ecrett seems to rank as one of the most pocket-friendly options among AI music generators.

## 4. Soundraw (Web): Customize Your Composition

Soundraw, the older sibling of Ecrett, works a bit differently than other AI music generators. On the Create Music page, extend the length, tempo, and mood of the track you'd like; you're kind of creating a guideline. The tracks that then come up work via tags—once again, something you can further modify—which show specific samples.

The sample tracks are completely original pieces fed by the producer team at Soundraw, and each sample can be played, edited, shared, and liked. As it was mentioned, with a free plan, you will be able to produce an unlimited number of tracks; if you want to download the song, then you will need to upgrade your membership to gain access among other features to editing the tempo and structure of music.

Depending on how you envision the dynamics and transitions from one section to another, you may modulate the energy for track segments among Low, Medium, High, and Very High—making it that much more personalized to your version of the song.

You can also create a duplication of sections of the song by clicking the plus under the segment you have highlighted on the sound wave. Remove a segment the same way by clicking the trashcan icon.

## 5. Boomy (Web): Add Custom Vocals to AI-Generated Songs and Release a “Single”

First off, Boomy will give you options to start you off: Electronic Dance, Rap Beats, Lo-Fi, Global Groove, Relaxing Meditation, and Custom for further personalization. You will then have options for mood off of your selected choice, after which the Boomy AI will create your base track. You will then be given numerous different ways to edit the created tracks, such as:

Vocals: You can add vocals into the track by singing directly in the app or uploading a WAV file. The auto-vocal option magically puts any spoken audio into a complete song; the custom vocal option uses the original audio track you ????'ve uploaded to record over your song.
Change Tempor or Density of tracks like Lead, Bass, Chords, and Drum.
That whole AI song is now sectioned off. Then, you can drag and move these sections to create new arrangements or even copy and delete them.
Instruments and Sounds: Select other instruments to be used in the song, filter instruments/sounds you don't want the AI to draw from in creating your track. You can further play around with the mixing to make it feel like it was recorded in a garage, outdoors, or even in a professional studio using Boomy.
Every time you make significant changes, Boomy regenerates a track so that it can be previewed. Then, save it to your profile once you are satisfied with the final product and download it for most non-commercial use cases, as well as some commercial purposes.

Boomy allows one to release tracks. This directs the track automatically to services like Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, etc. We recommend our readers go through the Boomy terms and conditions for a detailed view of how the money is divided.

If you're using the free tier plan, then you are permitted to release 5 songs. However, if you have the premium tier plan, you may be able to release 20 songs, and if you have a pro plan, then any number of songs can be released.

## 6. Beatbot (Web): Generate Music From a Text Prompt

What really sets Beatbot apart from other AI music generators is the whole track generated from a descriptive text prompt. You can just load up the website, slap in that description into the text box, and click Generate. Stumped for ideas? The Surprise me button will input a random text prompt and generate a track for you.

Be as specific as possible with the mood, genre, instruments, and other elements when considering what to put in the text box. Another remarkable element of this tool is its ability to generate vocals. You can do this either altogether in the text prompt or separately by clicking the info icon, then selecting a type of vocal, followed by gender, and then genre type:.

If you don't like what you hear, you can click the edit icon and then revise the text. You will be creating another track, rather than editing the existing one, so there's a good chance that you will lose something you like. You can always simply click Start again, either adding to the text, or writing something entirely different. It's worth noting that the generated track is fairly short: most of the time it's about four lines of vocals.

If one does not want the lyrics, he may always edit them; the edit option is in the bottom right of the video. Once one is satisfied, he can download the audio, video, and instrumental for free and share the link.

## Beware: With AI Music, Copyright Is Tricky

Once you’ve made your AI-generated music track, you’ll probably want to download it and use it in your projects. Depending on the app you chose and the plan you bought, you should be covered by their terms and services. However, as Google recently found out, copyrights can be tricky with AI-made music. So, you should keep these in mind when generating songs.
